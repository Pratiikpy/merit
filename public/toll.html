Verified Citation Toll

The gate under pay-per-crawl. Cloudflare, TollBit and ProRata pay a publisher whether or not the AI's answer actually used their work. Merit is the neutral check they call first: give it a claim and the cited source, it returns a signed release-or-refuse verdict, and your rail pays only when the citation genuinely holds.

Merit does not move the money — it returns the signed verdict your rail settles on. The same verificationId links it to your payment, the /proof ledger and the on-chain hook.

Drop it into any rail

// before releasing a per-citation toll, ask Merit if the citation actually holds
const gate = await fetch("https://onmerit.xyz/api/toll/verify", {
  method: "POST", headers: { "content-type": "application/json" },
  body: JSON.stringify({ claim, citedPassage, tollUsdc: 0.002, publisher })
}).then(r => r.json());

if (gate.receipt.decision === "release") payPublisher(gate.receipt.released);  // verified — pay
else hold(gate.receipt.reason);                                     // unsupported — $0
